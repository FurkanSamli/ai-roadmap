## g5 | ethics | Ethics, Safety & Law | Ethics & Safety
> Not just what we can do, but what we should.
? Using a powerful tool well: is it fair, safe, legal, whose data trained it? If you'll work in Europe, these are part of daily work.
- [1] Bias & fairness
  > Biases from data, fairness metrics
  ? A model trained on a company's past hiring of mostly men may unfairly reject women; a model mirrors its data.
- [1] Societal impact
  > Jobs, deepfakes, disinformation, energy use
  ? Debates on how AI affects jobs, trust in information and the environment.
- [2] Transparency & documentation
  > Model cards, datasheets for datasets
  ? Writing down clearly what a model was trained on and where it works well or badly.
- [2] Privacy
  ? Building AI while protecting personal data.
  - [1] Personal data protection & anonymization
    ? Removing or masking names, addresses and other identifying details from data.
  - [3] Differential privacy
    ? Adding controlled noise so that no individual's data can be inferred from the result, with a mathematical guarantee.
  - [3] Federated learning
    ? Data stays on the device; only model updates are shared.
- [2] Model security
  ? Attacks on AI systems and how to defend against them.
  - [2] Adversarial attacks
    ? Fooling a model with changes people barely notice, like a few stickers making a stop sign look like a speed limit sign.
  - [2] Prompt injection & jailbreaks
    ? Trying to make an LLM break its rules with instructions hidden in a web page or document; the hottest security issue for LLM apps.
  - [3] Data poisoning & backdoors
    ? Slipping malicious examples into training data to teach the model a hidden behavior.
  - [3] Model stealing & membership inference
    ? Trying to copy a model just by querying it, or to tell whether a specific person's data was used in training.
- [3] AI safety
  ? Keeping increasingly capable AI systems doing what people intend, and under control.
  - [3] Alignment
    ? Getting the model to do what people actually mean, not just what they literally said.
  - [3] Mechanistic interpretability
    ? Opening up a neural network and reverse-engineering which neurons represent which concepts.
  - [3] Red teaming & capability evaluations
    ? Deliberately trying to misuse a model before release to find its weak spots.
  - [3] Scalable oversight
    ? Research on how to supervise an AI on tasks where it's better than the humans checking it.
- [2] Law & regulation
  ? The laws and rules governing AI; directly relevant if you work in Germany.
  - [2] EU AI Act
    > Risk-based regulation
    ? The EU law classifying AI systems by risk: some uses are banned, high-risk ones face strict rules.
  - [2] GDPR & KVKK
    ? Europe's (GDPR) and Turkey's (KVKK) personal data protection laws; every data project must comply.
  - [2] Copyright & data licenses
    ? Which data can you train on, and who owns what the model produces? Still debated questions.
  - [2] Open model licenses
    > Apache 2.0, MIT, model-specific licenses
    ? Whether you can use an “open” model in a commercial product depends on its license.
- [2] Responsible AI & governance
  ? How companies oversee AI, who is accountable and which processes they follow.
