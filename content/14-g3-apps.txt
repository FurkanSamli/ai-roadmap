## g3 | apps | AI Application Domains | Applications
> Where AI is actually used across industries.
? Where do all these methods pay off? This cluster shows real uses by industry, and helps you think about which area you want to head into.
- [2] Automotive & mobility
  ? A field Ingolstadt sits right in: cars understanding their surroundings, helping drivers and one day driving themselves.
  - [2] Driver assistance systems (ADAS)
    ? Systems that help the driver, like lane keeping, emergency braking and adaptive cruise control.
  - [3] Autonomous driving stack
    > Perception, prediction, planning, control
    ? The car understanding what it sees, predicting what others will do, planning its path and turning the wheel.
  - [3] Safety validation & scenario testing
    ? How do you prove an AI is safe over millions of kilometers? With simulation and standards.
  - [2] Traffic & fleet optimization
    ? Predicting and improving traffic flow, charging station use or delivery routes.
- [2] Industry 4.0 & manufacturing
  ? Machines, sensors and AI working together in factories.
  - [2] Predictive maintenance
    ? Predicting from sensor data when a machine will fail, before it does.
  - [2] Visual quality inspection
    ? Automatically catching defective parts on the production line with cameras.
  - [3] Digital twin
    ? Building a virtual copy of a factory or product and trying changes there first.
- [2] Healthcare & medicine
  ? From diagnosis to drug development, one of the fields where AI can matter most and needs the most care.
  - [2] Medical image analysis
    ? Detecting findings like tumors in X-rays, MRIs and pathology images.
  - [3] Drug discovery
    ? Quickly narrowing millions of molecules down to the promising ones.
  - [3] Clinical decision support
    ? Giving doctors risk alerts and suggestions based on patient data.
- [3] AI for science
  > Protein structure prediction (AlphaFold), materials science, climate modeling
  ? AI speeding up scientific discovery, like predicting protein shapes or suggesting new materials.
- [2] Finance
  > Fraud detection, credit scoring, risk modeling
  ? Catching suspicious transactions instantly and estimating credit risk; explainability can be a legal requirement here.
- [2] E-commerce, media & search
  > Recommenders, search ranking, advertising
  ? The systems deciding what you watch, what you buy and which result you see first.
- [2] Cybersecurity
  > Intrusion and anomaly detection
  ? Spotting attacks early by catching unusual behavior in network traffic and system logs.
- [2] Software development
  > Coding assistants, automated testing, debugging
  ? AI tools that write, explain and debug code; something a CS student will meet every day.
- [2] Education
  ? Personal tutors that adapt to a student's level, and automatic feedback.
- [3] Energy, climate & agriculture
  ? Forecasting electricity demand, managing renewables, monitoring crop health from satellites.
- [2] Law & public sector
  ? Analyzing long documents, reviewing contracts and speeding up public services; bias risks are critical here.
