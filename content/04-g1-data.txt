## g1 | data | Data | Data
> A model is never better than its data: “garbage in, garbage out”.
? In real projects most time goes not into writing models but into finding, cleaning and preparing data.
- [1] Types of data
  > Tabular, text, image, audio, time series, graph
  ? A spreadsheet is structured data; photos, audio and free text are unstructured. Each kind has its own tools.
- [1] Data collection
  ? Finding or creating the data that will feed the model.
  - [1] Public datasets
    > Kaggle, Hugging Face Datasets, UCI
    ? Datasets others collected and shared; the first stop for learning and benchmarking.
  - [2] APIs & web scraping
    > requests, BeautifulSoup, Scrapy
    ? Pulling data from websites or services with code; you must respect terms of use and the law.
  - [2] Data labeling
    > Label Studio, CVAT; inter-annotator agreement
    ? People writing the right answer on data: “this image has a cat”. The fuel of supervised learning, and its most expensive part.
- [1] Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
  > Seeing distributions, correlations and anomalies
  ? Getting to know the data before modeling: draw charts, spot weird values, ask questions.
- [1] Data cleaning
  ? Fixing wrong, missing and inconsistent data; boring, but the step that affects results most.
  - [1] Missing values
    > Dropping or imputing
    ? Deciding what to do with empty cells: drop the row, fill with the mean, or predict the value.
  - [1] Outliers
    ? Values far from the rest, like a 250-year-old customer. You must decide whether it's an error or real.
  - [1] Duplicates & errors
    ? The same record twice, misspelled categories, mixed-up units.
- [2] Feature engineering
  ? Turning raw data into numbers the model can understand and use; on tabular data often more important than the model.
  - [1] Categorical encoding
    > One-hot, label, target encoding
    ? Turning words like “Ingolstadt, Munich, Berlin” into numbers a model understands.
  - [1] Scaling
    > Min-max normalization, z-score standardization
    ? Bringing salary (thousands) and age (tens) to the same scale so big numbers don't dominate.
  - [2] Feature selection
    > Filter, wrapper, embedded methods
    ? Picking the columns that actually help out of hundreds; the model gets faster and memorizes less.
  - [2] Feature creation
    > Date, text and interaction features
    ? Extracting new clues from existing data: “is it a weekend?” from a date, “price per square meter” from two columns.
- [1] Data splitting
  ? Splitting data so the model can be tested on data it has never seen.
  - [1] Train, validation, test
    ? Learn on train, tune on validation, get the final grade on test. Tuning on the test set is like cheating.
  - [2] Data leakage
    > Test information leaking into training; the sneakiest bug
    ? If the model sees the answer key before the exam, it scores great but collapses in the real world; very hard to notice.
  - [2] Splitting time series
    ? A model that predicts the future must be trained on the past and tested on the future; shuffling would be cheating.
- [2] Imbalanced data
  > Oversampling, undersampling, SMOTE, class weights
  ? If only 1 in 1000 transactions is fraud, a model saying “all clean” scores 99.9% and is useless. Ways to fix that.
- [2] Data augmentation
  > Rotating, cropping, adding noise, paraphrasing text
  ? Multiplying your data with small changes: flipping and cropping the same cat photo to make new examples.
- [3] Synthetic data
  > Generating data with simulation or generative models
  ? When real data is scarce or private, generating realistic data by computer; simulation is common in self-driving.
- [3] Data engineering
  ? Building the pipelines that move data from sources, clean it and feed models regularly.
  - [2] ETL / ELT pipelines
    ? Extract, transform, load. The data jobs that run every night look like this.
  - [2] Data formats
    > CSV, JSON, Parquet, Arrow
    ? How data is stored on disk. Parquet is much faster and smaller than CSV for big tables.
  - [3] Big data tools
    > Apache Spark, Kafka
    ? Processing data too big for one computer across many machines (Spark) and moving streaming data (Kafka).
  - [3] Data warehouse & data lake
    > BigQuery, Snowflake, lakehouse
    ? Big stores where all of a company's data is collected; the tidy kind is a warehouse, the raw kind a lake.
- [3] Data quality & governance
  > Validation, lineage, documentation
  ? Tracking where data came from, who changed it and whether it's correct.
