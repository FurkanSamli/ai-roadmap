- [2] ROS
  > Robot Operating System
  ? The standard software framework for robots; it carries messages between sensors, motors and algorithms.
- [3] Perception & sensor fusion
  > Camera, LiDAR, radar
  ? Combining data from different sensors into a reliable picture of the surroundings.
- [3] SLAM
  > Simultaneous localization and mapping
  ? A robot mapping an unknown place while also working out where it is on that map.
- [3] Motion planning & kinematics
  ? Working out how a robot arm or vehicle reaches its goal without hitting obstacles.
- [3] Control theory
  > PID, model predictive control (MPC)
  ? Sending constant small corrections to motors to keep a system where you want it, like cruise control.
- [3] Simulation & Sim2Real
  > Isaac Sim, MuJoCo, Gazebo
  ? Training a robot millions of times in a virtual world, then transferring it to the real one.
- [3] Autonomous vehicles
  ? Among the most complex robotic systems, where perception, prediction, planning and control all work together.
- [3] Vision-language-action models (VLA)
  ? Models that understand an instruction like “pick up the red cup” and drive the robot directly.
